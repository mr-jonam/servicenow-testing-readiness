The decision in focus

What evidence would explain the effect of a workflow change to its owner?

ServiceNow product owners, workflow owners and QA leads discussing upgrades or configuration changes.

An orientation resource for a focused first conversation. Results reflect your answers and assumptions, not observed system behaviour.

What you can take away

  • Separate platform automation discussion from end-to-end workflow acceptance.
  • Consider requester, fulfiller and integration perspectives.
  • Prepare a concise upgrade or change discussion brief.

Before you use this guide

Does this generate ATF tests?

No. It frames the evidence discussion and does not access or configure an instance.

Is the linked documentation current for my instance?

Not necessarily. The reference is release-specific; confirm the correct family with the platform owner.
