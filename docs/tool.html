Use category-level information. Do not enter confidential details.

1. The expected requester and fulfiller outcomes are clear.
2. The target release family and change category are known.
3. Role and approval perspectives have been discussed.
4. External handovers and notifications have been considered.
5. The limits of available automation evidence are understood.
6. An owner can decide on unresolved change questions.
